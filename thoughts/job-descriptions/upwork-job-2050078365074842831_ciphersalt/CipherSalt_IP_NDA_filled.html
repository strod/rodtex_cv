CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of this  4th  day of  May , 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between CipherSalt Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and the undersigned individual or entity (the "Contractor").

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means all non-public, proprietary, or sensitive information disclosed by the Company to Contractor. This specifically includes, but is not limited to: (a) the proprietary "Salt Score" methodology and related algorithms; (b) marketing capital allocation models; (c) integrated datasets derived from Amazon Marketing Cloud, Snowflake, Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM), and procurement metrics; and (d) all software code, "Agentic" UI/UX designs, and Vercel-based prototypes developed for the Company.

2. OWNERSHIP AND ASSIGNMENT OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

2.1 Work Made for Hire. Contractor acknowledges that all original works of authorship made by Contractor within the scope of the project which are protectable by copyright are "works made for hire," as defined in the United States Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 101).

2.2 Present Assignment. To the extent any Work Product (including code, quants, models, and documentation) does not qualify as a "work made for hire," Contractor hereby irrevocably and presently assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to such Work Product, effective immediately upon creation.

2.3 Power of Attorney. Contractor irrevocably designates and appoints the Company and its duly authorized officers as Contractor's agent and attorney-in-fact, to act for and on Contractor's behalf to execute and file any documents necessary to protect the Company's rights in the Work Product.

3. WARRANTY OF ORIGINALITY AND NON-CONFLICT

Contractor represents and warrants that they are not currently engaged in, nor have they previously developed, any project or technology that utilizes a proprietary alpha or composite score for supplier performance derived from the integration of marketing data, procurement data, and capital allocation logic. Signing this Agreement serves as a formal declaration that Contractor has no pre-existing claims to, or ongoing development of, substantially similar Intellectual Property.

4. MANDATORY DESTRUCTION OF MATERIALS

Upon termination or conclusion of the project, Contractor shall: (a) cease all use of Confidential Information; (b) irretrievably delete and destroy all electronic copies of Company data from all local machines, cloud environments, and repositories; and (c) provide written certification of such destruction to the Company within five (5) business days.

5. GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any dispute arising from this Agreement shall be adjudicated exclusively in the state or federal courts located in the State of Delaware.
